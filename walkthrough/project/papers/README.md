# Papers

This folder holds five open-access papers on Swiss housing and rental
markets. All five licenses permit redistribution, so the PDFs ship in
this public repository. The Gehriger paper is licensed under CC
BY-NC-ND 4.0: it may be shared but **must not be modified**; leave the
PDF untouched.

| File | Citation | DOI | License | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kauer Lutz Kaufmann 2025 Housing after Redevelopment.pdf | Kauer, Lutz & Kaufmann 2025, "Housing after redevelopment: Where and to what buildings do displaced residents move?", *Urban Studies* 63(4) | 10.1177/00420980251376156 | CC BY 4.0 | Tracks where residents displaced by building redevelopment end up. |
| Lutz Wicki Kaufmann 2024 Creating Inequality in Access to Public Transit.pdf | Lutz, Wicki & Kaufmann 2024, "Creating inequality in access to public transit? Densification, gentrification, and displacement", *Environment and Planning B* 51(9) | 10.1177/23998083241242883 | CC BY-NC 4.0 | Asks whether densification near transit displaces poorer households. |
| Gehriger 2023 Political Alienation Tenants Mass Cancellations Basel.pdf | Gehriger 2023, "Operationalizing the problem of political alienation for housing studies: Tenants experiencing mass cancellations of rental contracts in Basel, Switzerland", *Housing Studies* 39(12) | 10.1080/02673037.2023.2238643 | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 | Studies tenants facing mass rental-contract cancellations in Basel. |
| Lacroix Bertrand 2024 Housing Trajectories of Asylum Migrants in Switzerland.pdf | Lacroix & Bertrand 2024, "From collective centres to private accommodation: housing trajectories of asylum migrants in Switzerland", *Housing Studies* 40(5) | 10.1080/02673037.2024.2344856 | CC BY 4.0 | Follows asylum migrants' paths from collective centres into housing. |
| Pagani Baur Binder 2021 Tenants Residential Mobility in Switzerland.pdf | Pagani, Baur & Binder 2021, "Tenants' residential mobility in Switzerland: the role of housing functions", *Journal of Housing and the Built Environment* 36(4) | 10.1007/s10901-021-09874-5 | CC BY 4.0 | Explains why Swiss tenants move, via the functions housing serves. |

name: grill-me
description: Stress-test a plan, design, or feature via relentless questioning. "Grill me."
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan, feature, design or refactor until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

**Ask the questions one at a time; use the question tool.**

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
